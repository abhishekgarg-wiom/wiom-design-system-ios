# Recharge belief model: approved customer rules

Only the owner-approved rows below apply to screen and copy work. Specific prices, eligibility and product configuration still require verified product truth.

Draft rows and historical offer explorations are retained in [drafts-not-in-use.md](drafts-not-in-use.md). **Not approved. Do not apply in screens or copy. Agents must never load that file for screen or copy work.**

## Approved model and vocabulary

| Rule | Guidance | Status |
|---|---|---|
| RB-01 | The unit of purchase is chosen days of net, not a recurring monthly liability. | Product doctrine locked; specific ladder is configuration |
| RB-02 | Customers already know mobile recharge. Teach what is new about choosing days of home net, not what recharge means. | Supplied customer research principle |
| RB-03 | Recharge is part of the product. Promotional benefits require a recharge action; do not invent automatic promotional validity extensions. | Product doctrine locked |
| RB-05 | Customer surfaces say रिचार्ज, नेट and घर का नेट. | Approved glossary portion only; the original mixed row and unapproved framing are retained in the unused drafts file. |
| RB-10 | Strengthen value through days and flexibility, while preserving price integrity. Do not manufacture a discount to escape a price anchor. | Product doctrine locked |

## Approved teaching boundary

| Rule | Guidance | Status |
|---|---|---|
| RB-11 | Before payment: teach what is paid today, what is received, true refund terms and hidden charges. No new commercial fact at payment. | Locked |
| RB-12 | Teach the recharge cycle during Welcome Internet: remaining days, expiry, extending and control. | Locked |
| RB-13 | Guided adoption settles remaining surprises. First recharge is a decision with no new concepts or lessons. | Locked |
| RB-14 | Verify understanding through observable behaviour. Clarify and continue; do not test intelligence or block the customer. | Locked |
